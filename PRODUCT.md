# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The site serves quick visitors such as recruiters, hiring managers, classmates, collaborators, and people who need to understand the owner in roughly twenty seconds. It also serves deep visitors who want to explore a connected archive of experience, projects, research, writing, education, skills, and personal interests.

## Product Purpose

This is a premium personal portfolio and long-term digital archive. It should introduce the owner professionally, make the CV and contact routes easy to find, and let visitors move from one area of work to another without feeling like they are browsing isolated CV sections.

## Brand Personality

Analytical, entrepreneurial, personal. The site should feel like a fintech product built on Bloomberg-grade data, with startup energy and enough warmth to show the person behind the profile.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic developer portfolios, neon crypto aesthetics, navy-and-gold banking clichés, excessive gradients, stock-template project grids, skill percentage bars, fake metrics or fake live data, strategy parameters of private research, invented biographies, and cluttered navigation.

## Design Principles

1. Show only real, verifiable facts. When information is missing, leave the field out rather than showing a placeholder.
2. Let the structure communicate depth: every entity can connect to related projects, articles, experiences, skills, and education.
3. Give quick visitors immediate orientation while preserving richer paths for deeper exploration.
4. Treat personal interests and photography as first-class parts of the archive.
5. Make content updates data-first so future entries do not require redesigning the site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, semantic headings, keyboard-friendly navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, descriptive image alt text, and touch targets suitable for mobile visitors.
